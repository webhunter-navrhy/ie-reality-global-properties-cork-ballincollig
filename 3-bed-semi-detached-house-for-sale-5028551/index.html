Description

Global Properties presents this exciting opportunity to acquire a 3-bedroom refurbishment project in the popular Killumney area of County Cork. The property is in need of complete renovation, making it an ideal project for a purchaser looking to create a home tailored to their own requirements. With its established location, proximity to Cork City and surrounding amenities, this property offers excellent potential for those seeking a renovation project, first home or investment opportunity.

Killumney is a small and peaceful village in County Cork, located just west of Cork City, in the parish of Ovens. Surrounded by beautiful countryside, it offers a quiet rural atmosphere while still being close to the city and nearby towns. The area is known for its strong sense of community, scenic landscapes, and easy access to local amenities, making Killumney a pleasant place to live and visit.
